Bidisha Bhattacharyya is an independent author, fiction writer, poet and creative professional from West Bengal, India.

Her literary interests span multiple genres, including horror, suspense, fantasy, romance, poetry, haiku, flash fiction and short stories.

Her writing journey began through online platforms such as YourQuote and Wattpad. Following her Class 12 examinations, writing gradually developed into a stronger passion, encouraging her to explore different forms of creative expression.

Through reading, practice and creative learning, she developed an interest in storytelling across different formats. Alongside her literary work, Bidisha has explored professional writing, copywriting, blogging and promotional content. Her creative journey continues through books, literary contributions and professional projects.
